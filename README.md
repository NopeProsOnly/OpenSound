# 🔊 OpenSound

> **The 100% Free, Private, and Open-Source Android Soundboard**  
> *Zero Ads • Zero Telemetry • 100% Offline • Peer-to-Peer Sharing*

OpenSound is a high-performance Android soundboard built with user privacy and simplicity at its core. Unlike standard soundboards cluttered with subscription banners, full-screen video ads, and analytics trackers, OpenSound runs completely offline on your device with no network permissions, no account requirements, and zero data collection.

### Highlights
- **No Ads or Tracking:** No banners, no popups, and no background telemetry.
- **Low-Latency Playback:** Instant audio retriggering with single-channel exclusive playback (pressing a new sound immediately stops the previous one).
- **Peer-to-Peer Sound Sharing:** Share and import soundboards offline without an internet connection using animated dynamic QR codes or Android NFC Beam (Host Card Emulation).
- **Full Tile Customization:** Assign custom sound files (WAV, MP3, OGG) or record voice clips directly inside the app.
